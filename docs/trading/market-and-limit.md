# Market and Limit orders

## Market orders

**Market**: "Buy/Sell at current market price". Where the exchange uses a price limit for market orders, what the orderbook cannot fill within that limit at once is cancelled, and the order ends partly filled. How far the limit goes, and the warning on a thin book: [Market orders](market-orders.md). On some exchanges, a market order that has not filled within the exchange’s time limit is canceled.

## Limit orders

**Limit**: Buy or sell at your price or better. It rests on the orderbook; a buy below the market (a sell above it) waits for the price to come to it.
