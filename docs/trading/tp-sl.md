# Take-profit and stop-loss on what you hold

*In the panel, Attach TP/SL in an order's details sets them on that order; setting them on a position from the panel comes in a later version.* You can put a take-profit and a stop-loss on a futures position you already have, however it was built (by several orders, in Hyperliquid's own app, by another tool), and on an order goodcryptoX placed, after it was sent or after it filled. goodcryptoX 2.0 offered the second; the first is new. Coins you hold on spot get theirs with spot trading.

The form shows **Stop Loss** first, with **SL type: Market | Trailing**, followed by its fields. **Follow TPs** is a stop-loss setting: with both exits on and a Market stop-loss, it is the stop-loss section's last row, after **SL trigger timeout** and above the Take Profit switch. **Take Profit** follows, with **TP type: Market | Limit | Trailing** and its fields. A Limit take-profit still disables the manual stop-loss and shows **not with a Limit TP**.

The DCA form uses the same TP type choice; its stop-loss is the bot's own ([DCA bot](../bots/dca.md)).

The take-profit and stop-loss block is the order form's, field for field ([how it reads](order-types.md#take-profit-and-stop-loss)): several targets with their shares, market, limit or trailing take-profit, a stop-loss by percent, by distance, at a price or as the loss you accept, Follow TPs, a trigger timeout. How each part behaves (sizes, levels, rounding, trailing, Edit) is in [Order primitives](../reference/orders/primitives.md#take-profit-and-stop-loss-tpsl).

## On a futures position

- **One per coin and account.** A second is refused and names the first ("ETH has a take-profit and stop-loss on the position already (x1): use Edit TP/SL"). On orders you can have as many as you like.
- **Sized from the position as the exchange reports it** when you set them: goodcryptoX reads your position and its entry price fresh. If the read fails you are told the position is unknown; it is never taken as zero.
- **Measured from your entry**, not from today's price, as the old form measured from the fill price: long 1.0 ETH bought at 3,000 with ETH at 3,300 and a take-profit of 5 % means "Sell at 3,150", which is already passed; goodcryptoX says "Your order will be triggered immediately" and sets it only if you send it anyway.
- **Reduce-only follows Settings › General › Trading**, read once when you set them, in the form and on the Telegram TP/SL buttons. Changing Settings later leaves these exits unchanged; we recommend keeping it on. In the API, `bracket.reduceOnly` in the request is used when sent; without it the exits are reduce-only, whatever Settings say.
- **They follow the position.** Every fill on the coin moves them: yours through goodcryptoX, one in the exchange's app or another tool, a bot's, the take-profits' own. The position they follow is built from those fills, each counted once by the exchange's own id: long 1.0 at 100 with take-profit 10 % and stop-loss 5 %; buy 1.0 more at 120 in Hyperliquid's app and the position is 2.0 at 110: the take-profit sells 2.0 at 121 and the stop-loss 2.0 at 104.5. Nothing is sent to the exchange for that unless a take-profit rests there (a limit take-profit is replaced at its new size: on Hyperliquid the old one is cancelled first, then the new one placed). Trailing levels and a stop-loss set as an expected loss follow the new entry too (see Order primitives).
- **A DCA bot on the coin** is warned about, never refused: a bot that holds nothing yet ("A DCA bot runs on ETH; if it opens a position, this take-profit and stop-loss follow it and can close the bot's coins"), or one that holds part of the position ("0.5 of this position belongs to the DCA bot; a stop on the whole position, or one that follows it, sells the bot's coins and distorts its PnL. Not recommended: use Attach TP/SL on your own order instead"). Send anyway to set them.

### When they end

| What happens | They | You hear |
|---|---|---|
| A goodcryptoX order closes the position (Close, your sell, the take-profit or the stop-loss itself) | end | nothing |
| The position is closed outside goodcryptoX (the exchange's app, another tool) | end | one alert: "ETH position closed outside goodcryptoX (x1); its take-profit and stop-loss ended" |
| The position changes side (long to short) | end, nothing is sent | one alert: "ETH position reversed (x1); …" |
| A liquidation, even of part of it | end | a page: "ETH position liquidated (x1); …" |

A later position on the coin does not bring them back; set new ones. Cancel ends them yourself.

### What goodcryptoX checks against the exchange

The exchange's own position is read when goodcryptoX starts (and once more 90 seconds later if that read disagrees), never moved into the take-profit and stop-loss: they follow the fills. Between starts it is not read again. A read that comes before a fill it already shows (your take-profit filled a moment ago and the fill is still on its way) changes nothing and alerts nobody. A read that fails is unknown, never zero. If the exchange still shows another size 90 seconds later, you are paged once and nothing is changed: press Cancel on the take-profit and stop-loss and set them again, and the new ones are sized from a fresh read. If it shows no position on two reads 90 seconds apart, at least 90 seconds after goodcryptoX's last fill there, the take-profit and stop-loss go to repair and you are paged; they are kept (never cancelled from a read): press Cancel on them if the position is really gone.

## On an order

**Attach TP/SL** (in the panel, the order's **Modify** with a switch turned on; for the API and agents, `POST /api/v1/exits`) on an order goodcryptoX placed that is open, partly filled or filled. On an order, the take-profit and stop-loss are placed **only once the order has filled in full**, sized from all its fills and levelled at their average price; nothing is placed for a part. **The consequence:** an order that fills only in part and then ends (you cancel it, its time in force ends, a market order's rest is cancelled) leaves what filled **without a take-profit and stop-loss**, and you are told once: "0.4 ETH filled, the order cancelled; no take-profit or stop-loss was placed." Set them on the position then if you want them (above). Refused, as goodcryptoX 2.0 refused them: an order of a bot (its take-profit and stop-loss are the bot's), a take-profit or stop-loss itself or a Close, an order that ended without a fill, and an order placed outside goodcryptoX (goodcryptoX 2.0 refused those too). An order that has them already: change them in its Modify (the API: Edit).

## Their own time in force

The block has its own **Time in force**, one for both (goodcryptoX 2.0 had none). The form uses the Settings › General › Trading default, one year unless you choose another finite duration or **Custom**. A preset duration counts from the moment the order filled in full (on a position, from when you set them), never from when you placed the order. Custom keeps its exact calendar deadline. The form and the API refuse a Custom date before the order’s own expiry (a market order’s is when it is sent); equality is allowed. A Custom date more than one year after it (on a position, or an order already filled, more than one year from now) is a warning: the form keeps its line red and offers Send anyway, and the API answers 422 until the warning is named in `overrides`. When it ends, every take-profit and stop-loss of the block still working is cancelled and you are told once ("Its take-profit and stop-loss reached their time in force and were cancelled."). The order's own time in force never applies to them. In the API: `bracket.timeInForce`, in milliseconds, or `bracket.cancelAt`, UTC milliseconds, never both. An older reader ignores `cancelAt`; new API and MCP submissions with neither field use one year, independent of the panel’s Settings value. Received durations or dates override that default. The API and MCP refuse a TP/SL duration over one year with HTTP 400 and the limit message; nothing is silently cut. Old journal rows without either keep their meaning and still live until cancelled.

## When one fires

A stop-loss or a market take-profit that fires is a market order, priced from the book when it is sent, at most 25 % from the best price (see [Market orders](market-orders.md)). **If the book fills only part of it, goodcryptoX sends the same stop-loss or take-profit again for the rest, and again, until nothing is left or the exchange refuses**; each send is priced from the book at its own moment, at most 25 % away, with no total limit. Each send for the rest shows as one more Stop Loss (or Take Profit) row under your order, "For the rest", and the order's mark counts it: "+2 SL", "+2 TP". While any of its orders is being sent it cannot be cancelled ("This stop has triggered; its order is being sent."): each ends within moments by itself. If the exchange refuses the sale because the account **holds less than it sells** ("Reduce only order would increase position" on futures; "Insufficient spot balance" on spot: a fill not reported yet, coins sold by hand, or held by another resting order of yours), the order does not end: it waits in **repair** with the exchange's words and you are paged once. When a fill changes what is held (a take-profit's fill reported late, any of the order's exits filling), goodcryptoX sends it once more by itself, for what is held now; if that is refused too, it waits for you. **Retry** checks your coins (or your position) and sells what is there, or, when there is nothing, ends the order refused with a **Not fully closed** row on the Dashboard. A part below 10 USDC beside a larger position **cannot be closed**: the order ends refused with the exchange's words, you are told, and a **Not fully closed** row appears on the Dashboard. Any other refusal (no liquidity within the bound, the price moved, an error goodcryptoX does not know) puts the order in **repair**, with the same Dashboard row: **Retry** sends the rest again when you choose. TP/SL set before this version keep the old rule: one order, the rest reported.

## On spot: a stop-loss beside a Limit take-profit

On spot a Limit take-profit rests on Hyperliquid and holds the coins it would sell: a second sell of them is refused ("Insufficient spot balance"), and Hyperliquid has no stop orders on spot. goodcryptoX 2.0 therefore allowed no stop-loss beside a Limit take-profit; goodcryptoX does, with its own stop-loss, watched on the last trade. When the price reaches it, goodcryptoX first cancels the Limit take-profit and sells at market only once Hyperliquid has confirmed the cancel, the same way the DCA bot does. What the take-profit sold before its cancel landed, as far as Hyperliquid had reported it, is not sold again: the stop-loss sells only what is left, or nothing when the take-profit filled in full. On futures a stop-loss beside a Limit take-profit is allowed too (goodcryptoX 2.0 allowed it there): nothing holds the coins on futures, so when the stop-loss fires goodcryptoX sells at once, reduce-only, and cancels the take-profit in the same step; when the take-profit fills first, the stop-loss is ended.

- **A small delay:** the stop-loss's sale leaves one request after the cancel, so its price may be a little worse.
- **In unlikely cases it may not execute:** if the exchange's answer to the cancel is lost, nothing is sold, the take-profit is shown in repair and you are paged; **Retry** on it sends the cancel again, and the sale follows its confirmation.
- **A fill reported after the cancel:** if the take-profit sold part at the last moment and the exchange reports that fill only after it confirmed the cancel, the sale goes out for the whole and is refused ("Insufficient spot balance"); the stop-loss waits in repair and you are paged once. When the fill is reported, goodcryptoX retries for the rest by itself, once, and the stop-loss ends with what it sold; under your order it shows as two Stop Loss rows, the refused one with its size and the exchange's words, then the one for the rest ("+2 SL"). If no fill comes (the coins were sold by hand), **Retry** checks your coins and sells what is there, or ends the stop-loss refused when there are none.

## From the order's page

The order's take-profit and stop-loss change in its **Modify**, the order form itself filled in with the order as it is now (see [Change an order](order-types.md#change-an-order)): the form's own take-profit and stop-loss block shows what is set now (a take-profit or stop-loss you cancelled is gone from it and never comes back), both switches off when there is none. Switch either on or off, change a type, add or remove a target with **+1 TP** and **−1 TP**, move a level or a size, tick **Reduce only** on or off for all of them (on futures; a new order takes Settings' default), and change the order itself in the same Save: both are applied or neither. The block changes while the order is open, and once it has filled until one of them fills, fires or a trailing take-profit starts to trail; after that each one still working changes on its own page: its level, a stop-loss's type (Market or Trailing, with its level or distance in the same change) and a trailing stop-loss's distance, and a trailing take-profit's type (to Market or Limit, with its level); never its size, which is the order's, nor Reduce only, which is one setting for all of them. **Save** asks once, "Apply these changes to the order?", with a line for each change, each level in its own unit ("Take profit 2 to 11 %"); **Do not ask again** turns the question off (Settings › General › Trading › Confirmation dialogs › Changing an order). A warning (a level already crossed, a date more than a year away) shows under the form, and **Save** sends it anyway. While the order is open nothing goes to the exchange: the take-profit and stop-loss are a plan used at the fill; if the exchange refuses the order's change, the plan before the Save comes back with the old order.

## Edit and Cancel

A change of the block sends it whole again, on a position or an order, at any time: what is still working is re-sized from what is held, a target that filled is never sent again (targets are positional: list every target still set, filled ones included, in their order; one you cancelled is no longer among them). A change of a take-profit's or stop-loss's kind (its type, trailing distance, minimum profit, trigger timeout or reduce-only) is one edit, as in goodcryptoX 2.0: the old one is cancelled, and the new one goes to the exchange once the cancel is confirmed. A trailing take-profit or stop-loss whose **distance alone** changes keeps trailing from where it got to: a stop that trailed up to 80 at 3 % (77.60) moves to 78.40 at 2 %, from the extreme it reached, not from the entry; one still waiting starts its wait again from its entry. A narrower distance can put the stop past the price: it then fires at the next price. A distance changed together with the minimum profit or reduce-only is a change of kind (above). Refused: once they have ended; and adding back one of the same kind that is still being cancelled on the exchange, which names it. **Cancel** ends every take-profit and stop-loss of the block still working.

Every take-profit and stop-loss block is a row of [My trades](trades.md), with its profit and loss.
