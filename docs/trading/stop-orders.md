# Stop Market and Stop Limit

## Stop Market

**Stop Market**: "Send Market order once trigger price is reached". goodcryptoX watches the last trade; when it reaches the trigger price, goodcryptoX sends a market order. A buy stop above the market is a breakout entry; a sell stop below the market is a stop-loss.

## Stop Limit

**Stop Limit**: "Send Limit order once trigger price is reached". The same, but at the trigger goodcryptoX places a limit order at your limit price.

## A stop sized in USDC

A stop you size in the quote currency, say 100 USDC, buys or sells 100 USDC worth when it triggers: its coins are worked out at the price that triggered it, a Stop Limit's at its limit price. Until then the coins shown are an estimate. Where the exchange takes a market buy in USDC, a buy stop goes out as 100 USDC. A sell stop sized in USDC sells more coins the lower it triggers: to sell what you hold, size it in the coin or use Max.
