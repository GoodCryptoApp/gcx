## 1. What goodcryptoX does

goodcryptoX is a trading program you run on your computer or server. It displays your accounts, sends orders, runs bots and delivers alerts. The exchange you connect holds your funds and executes your trades. We cannot see your panel or trading keys, and we do not hold, receive or control your funds.

You can open the panel from a phone, or from a computer away from your server, by pairing that device. A browser device is set to **Can trade**, which may do everything you can do at your computer except making the move file, or to **Read-only**, which only looks. You change the switch, or remove the device, in Settings › Devices. A passkey synced by Apple or Google carries its switch to your other devices in that account.

Apps and your own programs can connect through our encrypted relay using a paired computer identity and their own access keys. Pairing grants no access to data or trading by itself; every request uses the key's current permissions. Removing a program connection prevents later requests; a command already accepted may still finish. If a reply is lost, check the result before trying again. Your relay connection depends on our relay, the connector, the installation and the network being available.

If you turn on **Connect directly at home** in Settings › Devices, goodcryptoX also opens directly at this computer's network addresses and at any address you save, without our relay. Its link stays the same until you turn the switch off, and anyone who has it and is on your network can add a device while it is on: keep it to yourself, and turn the switch off and on again for a new link. A device you add there has the same switch, Can trade or Read-only. A Can-trade device may do everything you can do at your computer except making the move file: trade and import, change settings, show and create access keys and change their limits, and add other devices. It stays signed in while you use it; one unused for 90 days must be added again, and you can remove any device at once in Settings › Devices. The switch is off until you turn it on.

## 3. Connecting an account

On Hyperliquid, your own wallet approves goodcryptoX’s API wallet and, if needed, our builder fee. The trading key is created and kept on your machine; it can trade but cannot withdraw. On an exchange that uses API keys, such as Binance, the key can do what you allow when you create it on the exchange; goodcryptoX never withdraws or transfers funds, and we recommend a key without withdrawals. Keep keys private and renew approvals when needed. Expiry, revocation or a changed key can prevent goodcryptoX from placing, cancelling or closing orders; orders already resting on the exchange remain there. On Binance, when a key with no IP addresses has placed no order for three weeks, goodcryptoX places a small order far from the price and cancels it seconds later, unless you turn this off; it can fill if the price moves that far, and Binance may still delete or limit such a key.

## 4. Our fee

Today our builder fee is **0.03%** of each filled trade placed through goodcryptoX on Hyperliquid, in addition to exchange fees; on spot it applies only to sells. Hyperliquid collects it and asks your wallet to approve it once per main account, never charging above your approved maximum. Settings shows the current rate and approved maximum; older approvals may have a higher maximum, and we may change our rate at our discretion by updating these terms. You can revoke approval on Hyperliquid, but it may then refuse your orders, stop-losses and closes until you approve again.

**On exchanges where goodcryptoX cannot collect its fee with the trade** the same 0.03%, less a code's discount, comes from prepaid credits on each filled trade goodcryptoX places, buys and sells. Credits are bought in USDC or USDT on the networks the app offers, counted one dollar each, or in another coin swapped to them in the same transaction by a third-party swap service at the price it quotes, or in GOOD (burned, at a discount), or in another coin on Solana that a third-party swap service swaps into $GOOD in the same payment, the $GOOD it guarantees burned at the same discount; $GOOD is valued at what buying it on our pool costs at the time of the payment. Credits added are the dollars we receive, or those a GOOD purchase costs. If the network drops a payment after its credits were added, we take those credits back, and your balance can go below zero. Credits are not money, earn nothing, are not transferable or refundable and belong to your installation. Every installation gets 10,000 USD worth of CEX volume there to begin with, without our fee; after it, our fee there comes from credits. When credits run out, running bots and every exit keep working, into a negative balance; no new opening order or bot starts there until your balance is above zero again.

Hyperliquid’s optional referral follows its rules and may earn us exchange fees. Separately, each installation can join one partner or referral code, never its own; it covers all accounts and cannot be changed or removed.

Partner codes share our fee between a discount and partner earnings under our agreement. A partner’s own key discounts their trades without earning a share. It links their payouts to the first installation using it; contact us to move that link.

Your three referral codes split 10% between the user’s fee discount and your earnings: 0/10, 5/5 or 10/0. Hyperliquid’s 5% discount gives a 0.028% fee. Earnings are a share of the fee we receive on each trade, counted before discounts, or of the amount paid for credits: a trade that brings us no fee earns nothing; GOOD purchases are burned and earn nothing; spending credits earns nothing again. Coin earnings keep their dollar value when earned.

Request payouts from 50 USD in USDC or USDT, chosen by us, on a network offered in the app. They arrive within 7 days; we pay the network fee. The address stays locked while a request waits; cancel before we start sending. We may change or end codes.

## 6. Trading risks

You can lose all the money you trade with. Leverage increases losses, and liquidation can affect your account beyond one position. Bots follow your settings, not your intentions or predictions.

goodcryptoX’s own stops, trails and waiting bot orders work only while it runs with working keys and market data. Power loss, sleep, a lost connection, updates, expiry or removal of an account can leave positions unprotected. Actions may execute when it resumes, at worse prices. Partial fills, refused orders or uncertain replies can also leave a position without its intended exit. Check your accounts and protect positions directly on the exchange when necessary.

If Binance does not answer an order, goodcryptoX checks the exchange within seconds and sends it again automatically, with the same order id, only after its checks show the first was not placed. Binance does not guarantee how late its matching engine may execute an order; a late first order and a retry can both execute. Check the exchange when an outcome is uncertain.

Software, networks and exchanges can fail. A leaked key can trade your account into losses. A vault’s leader is responsible for trading followers’ money. Anyone allowed to use your trading keys, computer, Telegram shortcuts or a paired phone can act with your funds. Start small, with money you can afford to lose.

A browser paired through our relay opens the panel through a page our server delivers. If that page, our servers or our web address were taken over, whoever did it could act on every Can-trade device opened in the meantime, with your funds, and read what a Read-only device shows. Set a device to Read-only if it should never trade, and remove one you lose or no longer use.

Over your home network goodcryptoX is served by your own computer, not by our server, usually as plain HTTP. Anyone who can read or change traffic on that network, or who sets up a false copy of an address you saved, can take a device's sign-in or show you a false panel, and then act as that device with your funds, create access keys and add devices that stay after you remove the first. It is the link and the devices you keep, not the network, that keep others out; goodcryptoX checks where a request comes from only as far as it can, and a proxy, VPN or router can pass that check. Do not forward its port to the internet. If a device is lost or you suspect someone else got in, remove the device, turn the switch off and on again for a new link, then check Devices and remove any device you did not add; revoke keys made meanwhile and any key that may have been copied. Trades already accepted and data already read cannot be undone.
