goodcryptoX’s own stops, trails and waiting bot orders work only while it runs with working keys and market data. Power loss, sleep, a lost connection, updates, expiry or removal of an account can leave positions unprotected. Actions may execute when it resumes, at worse prices. Partial fills, refused orders or uncertain replies can also leave a position without its intended exit. Check your accounts and protect positions directly on Hyperliquid when necessary.

Software, networks and exchanges can fail. A leaked key can trade your account into losses. A vault’s leader is responsible for trading followers’ money. Anyone allowed to use your trading keys, computer, Telegram shortcuts or a paired phone can act with your funds. Start small, with money you can afford to lose.
