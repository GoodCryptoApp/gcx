# Trade screen: your lists

The Trade screen brings together your positions, manual orders and bots across your venue accounts. Switch lists with **Positions · Orders · Bots**; Orders keeps **Open / History**. Rows open their details in the list pane without leaving the Trade screen. Bot rows show the position as the Positions list's Size cell: the coin amount in its side's colour above the approximate USD value in grey, at the same sizes.

## The tabs

- **Positions**: open positions on your venue accounts, sorted by absolute value at the current mark price, largest first by default. Long and short positions are compared by value without their sign. Positions with zero size leave the list, except a position liquidated or closed by auto-deleveraging in the last 24 hours: it stays greyed at the bottom, with its **Liquidated** or **ADL** tag and time. A position still open after a partial liquidation has a **Partly liquidated** tag.
- **Orders → Open** (every status word, and what to press: [What the status words mean](../concepts/states.md#orders)): orders resting on the venue, triggers goodcryptoX watches for you (a stop shows "Waiting" until it fires), orders being sent, orders waiting for a delayed start, and orders whose answer from the exchange was lost ("Checking with Hyperliquid" for about two minutes, then "Unknown" with the reason in a sentence, such as "The stop fired but its order could not be filled"; see [When goodcryptoX gets no answer](../trading/lost-answer.md)). Only manual orders appear here, including orders placed outside goodcryptoX. A bot’s parent and child orders appear only in its Bot details pane.
- **Bots**: a **DCA | Grid** switch picks the kind of bot the list shows (your choice is remembered); running and repair bots come before stopped ones. The status dot is first, just before the time; the Time heading starts where the times start. Rows show the instrument and account, name with type and cycle beneath it, averaging progress, signed USD position value, PnL and action icons. Copy and Close position live in the details. Sort through the Time, Progress, Position, R, U and APR column headings; running bots stay above ended bots. A Grid row shows **Trades** where a DCA row shows its averaging progress. See [the Bots list](../bots/dca-running.md#the-bots-list) and [Running a Grid](../bots/grid-running.md).
- **Orders → History**: manual orders that have ended, and every liquidation or auto-deleveraging the exchange made on the account, one row per exchange order, whose Type reads **Liquidation** or **ADL** once, in red, with no actions; its details say what the bots on the coin were charged and what is yours ("Bots: 4 · Yours: 1"). goodcryptoX keeps every error and every cancelled order. All includes filled, cancelled and errored orders; choose Filled to hide errors and cancellations. Older history loads as you scroll; a filled order's time is the exchange's.

Open orders shows each of goodcryptoX's orders as its order engine holds it (sending, open, closed), and the orders placed elsewhere that rest now; Cancel on one of goodcryptoX's rows cancels it at the exchange and says "Cancel requested" until the exchange confirms. Cancel on an order placed elsewhere cancels it at the exchange too. Positions come from the exchange (size, entry, mark, unrealized PnL, liquidation price, margin, leverage), from Hyperliquid's live account updates (about every 5 seconds) while the list is open, and read again when you open it, every minute and within a second of a fill, in case the live updates stop; when the position opened and its realized PnL since it was last flat come from the account's fills that Hyperliquid lists, so for a position older than those the list counts from the oldest one listed. History shows goodcryptoX's ended orders, newest first.

## Columns

All row figures, including the book and Trades, keep full integer digits rather than k/M abbreviations. Instrument precision and the small-value number rules stay the same. Only the named Dashboard totals and holdings use compact figures.

**Positions** has **Instrument, Size, Margin, Liq. price, Entry / Mark and PnL**. Size shows the signed coin quantity above its approximate USD value. Margin shows the margin mode, then the leverage (**Isol. 1x**, **Cross 5x**), above the amount in the collateral coin; the position and bot details write the pair the same way. Liq. price includes the risk bar; Entry / Mark shows the two prices. PnL shows unrealized profit or loss above the exchange's return on equity (**ROE**); either can be selected for sorting. A position or an order a bot holds carries the grey **Bot** label beside its size; hover it on a position for your share and the bots'.

**Orders**, in both Open and History, has **Time, Instrument, Type, Size and Status**. Type is two lines: the order's type (with its attached TP / SL marks) and then **at price**, the price the order waits on: a stop's trigger price, a stop limit's trigger price until it triggers and then its limit price, a limit's price, a market order's expected price after a “~” (a filled market order has no second line). Size is the coin quantity in the side's colour with its approximate value in USD under it, as one figure, and its sort control sorts by value. Where Size folds into Type (a narrower list, a phone) Type's second line reads **size × price** instead. A sorted heading is marked by its triangle alone, never underlined. History uses the filled quantity and shows the average fill price above **Filled** or **Partly filled** in Status. A parent order's type keeps its attached TP / SL marks. Known trigger refusals read **Error** with the reason, while retaining their repair actions.

Instrument stays visible, including when the list is scoped to the screen's pair. The columns share the list's width evenly: each column is as wide as what it shows, and the space left over goes equally between them. A column folds only when the columns no longer fit the list pane: in Orders, Size joins Type (its head and sort stay, its figure moves to Type's second line); in Bots, Position joins Progress, and in the narrowest list the instrument joins the bot's name; Positions hides Entry / Mark first, then Margin. A cell never shows more than two lines: a folded cell shows its own column on the first line and the folded one on the second, and its heading names both, each with its own sort arrows (**Progress · Position**). A cell's text that does not fit ends in “…” or can be scrolled. Row actions sit in their own last column, always visible, unless the list is at its narrowest: there they open by swiping on a phone, or under the pointer in a narrow desktop pane. An order's row has no Modify: an order changes in its details (its **Modify**, or a pencil for one value).

**On a phone** the lists keep what matters and drop what is only nice to have. Orders and History keep the size and drop its approximate USD value; the order row keeps its two lines at every width. Bots shows R and U (APR is in the bot's details) and the position's size without its USD value. Positions keeps everything it shows. The filter row stays on one line: when there is no room, **Cancel all** is hidden first, then the account's name is shortened with “…”. The headings show only the columns the rows show.

A partly filled order is still open. Only partial fills show a thin fill bar under the size.

Position rows show unrealized PnL; position details show the unrealized PnL at the top right and, in their rows, the realized PnL since the position was last flat and the funding since it opened. Bot rows stack realized PnL including funding, unrealized PnL and the runtime's APR. Funding paid counts negative and funding received positive. Prices and sizes follow the instrument's own rule (see [How numbers are shown](../reference/number-display.md)); leverage reads 3x.

## The DCA list

A row's names and figures carry no tooltip (sizes, prices, values, PnL); an instrument, account or bot name cut short with "…" shows in full on hover or tap; figures retain full integer digits, with no k/M shortening and no cents tooltip. A dot is green for a bot that runs (**Running**, **Waiting** for its entry, **Cooldown**, **Stopping**), white for **Launching** (a scheduled start), grey for an ended bot (**Completed** at its take profit, stop loss or end, **Stopped** when you stopped it), red for **Error**, or yellow for **Needs repair** and for a running bot with a failed averaging order. Hover it for its status, and for an ended bot why it ended ("Take Profit triggered", "Manual cancel"). An active repair takes precedence over a failed averaging level; the failed count stays red in brackets. Stopped and terminal-error figures are grey, except the unrealized PnL of a position still open. No rows or trading state are changed by their color.

The instrument and small derivative label share the first line; the account is below, beside its exchange icon. Spot has no label; perpetuals say **Perp**, and a HIP-3 dex uses its own name. A position's second-line amount includes its quote unit and shows its value at the last traded price. Details still identifies the average entry price separately. The list uses the rows already served; it adds no market-data subscription.

Progress reads **averaging 2/6** and **cycle 3**, with any failed averaging count in red brackets. **R**, **U** and **APR** are the three PnL sort keys in the header (on a phone, R and U); one arrow marks the selected key. R includes funding, U is the open unrealized PnL, and APR is served by the runtime. Other headers sort their first line, as their tooltips say. On a phone, swipe to reveal Time and the action icons.

The row's icons are **Stop**, **Chart**, and **Fix failed** when that existing action is available for failed averaging orders. Chart stays reachable on failed rows at every width. The same icons appear beside their words in Details. Chart selects this account/instrument and draws the bot's entry, averaging and exit orders through its existing details read, while keeping the Details pane closed. Changing the form, account or instrument, replacing the chart context or leaving the page clears that selection.

The liquidation price in Bot details is the exchange's own figure. When averaging orders remain, its line says **Averaging orders still to fill will move it**. There is no estimate from goodcryptoX.

## Orders placed outside goodcryptoX

Orders you place on the same account somewhere else (in the venue's own app, or with another tool) show in **Open orders** while they rest, with a subtle **ext** tag; **In-app / All** lets you leave them out. goodcryptoX watches them live but does not keep them: they are not written in its ledger, they never alert you, and goodcryptoX never cancels or changes them by itself. **Cancel** on one of their rows cancels it at the exchange when you press it: one request by the exchange's order id, sent once, with no builder fee; the row leaves the list when the exchange confirms, or when it says the order was already filled or cancelled. If the exchange does not answer, the cancel is not sent again; goodcryptoX reads your open orders again and the row follows what the exchange reports. When one fills or is cancelled it leaves the list; its trades are not in goodcryptoX's history yet. Orders already resting when you open the account in the panel are read from the exchange as it opens (those on the main exchange; an order on another Hyperliquid dex shows once the exchange next reports it).

## Filters and sorting

Positions, Orders and Bots share one header. Orders keeps **Open / History**. Orders and History start with a source choice, then the scope pairs run from left to right: symbol, account, **Filled / All** (History only; Open does not show it). The source choice is **In-app / All** when outside orders exist: **In-app** is everything placed through goodcryptoX (by you, your bots and your API keys), **All** adds the orders placed outside it. Once you have an API key it reads **In-app / Agents / All**: **Agents** is the part of In-app placed through a key (an AI agent or a program, by MCP or API). Positions and Bots show **Agents / All** the same way, and only when you have a key. From a key's page in Settings, **Open in lists** shows that key's orders: the key's name takes the place of Agents, with a cross that goes back to All. A choice that does not apply to a list is not shown. In every pair **All** is on the right. History starts at All, including errors and cancellations; Filled hides those rows. History remembers its choice.

Each scope pair is two words beside a thin line, not a pill: the chosen word stands out, the other is grey. Pills are for choosing a kind of list (Open / History, Positions / Orders / Bots). The symbol and account pairs always remain. If room is short, Filled drops first. A hidden pair applies All while preserving its saved choice for a wider screen. The source choice stays. External orders carry a subtle **ext** tag; Positions do not.

**Search** is the word “Search” with a magnifier at the right end of the tab row (Positions | Orders | Bots), above the filter row, as in goodcryptoX: no box, no border. Click the word and type; the list filters as you type (after a short pause). On a phone only the magnifier shows in the tab row until you tap it. With text the magnifier becomes a cross that clears it. It also finds order-type words such as “stop” and “trailing”. There are no Type or Filters dropdowns. Sort by a column's available sort control; your choices are remembered per list in this browser.

When your filters hide rows the list has, the empty line is followed by **Reset filters**, centred under it, which sets every filter back to All. A list that has no rows at all shows only its line (“No active orders”, “No positions”, “No bots”); there is nothing to reset.
Search reads the whole stored list, including rows that have not yet loaded on screen. It finds instrument and account names, bot names and order-type words such as “stop” and “trailing”. Results load in pages; **Show all results** loads the remaining matches. **Cancel all**, **Stop all** and **Close all** act on the entire matching set, including matching rows beyond the page on screen.

There are no Type or Filters dropdowns. An empty filtered list offers **Reset filters**. Sort through the available column controls; pressing the selected control reverses its direction. Open positions, open orders and running bots stay ahead of closed or ended rows whichever direction you choose: the sort applies inside those fixed groups. Positions starts with absolute value, largest first; Orders and Bots start with Time, newest first. Your choices are remembered per list in this browser.

An account removed with **Keep trading history** keeps its history under All, marked Account removed, with no trading actions. Restoring the same account removes that mark.

## Cancel all and Stop all

At the right end of the filter row, **Open orders** has **Cancel all** and **Bots** has **Stop all**. Each acts on exactly what the list shows with its filters as they are: the visible symbol and account scopes, the source choice and search. Cancel all is absent from History. It asks first, with the count: "Cancel 7 orders?" or "Stop 3 bots?" (only bots still running count). Confirmed, each order gets its own cancel, as if you pressed Cancel on its row, orders placed outside goodcryptoX included when the list shows them. Bot orders are absent from this list and never counted or cancelled here. Use Stop all or the bot’s Stop for those; each bot gets its own Stop, which cancels its orders and closes its position only if its "Close pos. on stop" says so. Nothing outside the filter is touched. A row goodcryptoX could not cancel or stop says why; if goodcryptoX cannot take them at the moment (it is starting, stopping or locked), the list says so and nothing was done. With the disk full each cancel and stop is taken and kept: it goes by itself once there is room. The same two are in the API (`POST /api/v1/orders/cancel-all`, `POST /api/v1/bots/stop-all`). goodcryptoX 2.0 had no cancel-all; the place at the end of the filter row is new.

**Close all** is the same on **Positions**: at the end of the filter row, acting on exactly the positions the list shows with its filters (the scope, the side, your search). It asks first with the count ("Close 3 positions?"; a position of no size is not counted), then closes each one as if you pressed Close on its row: the position is read again from the exchange when it is sent and closed at market, so one that closed meanwhile is not closed again. When a bot holds all or part of one of those positions (a running bot, or a bot you stopped without closing that still holds its position), the question names how many positions are bot-held and says that **Close all** also stops their bots.

**Close mine** closes your positions and, on a shared one, only your part: the exchange's size when it is sent less what the bots hold, so the bots' parts stay and the bots keep running; if your part is under the exchange's minimum order value its row says so and nothing is sent for it. **Close all** closes your positions and parts the same way, then stops each of those bots with its position closed (the bot cancels its take-profit and stop-loss, books its profit and ends; a stopped bot is force-closed).

The action button is on the left and the way out is on the right. When every matched position belongs wholly to bots, the buttons are **Close all** and **Cancel**. On a single position partly held by bots, **Close all** closes it and stops its bots, while **Close mine** leaves their parts running. On a position wholly held by bots, the buttons are **Close anyway** and **Cancel**. The cross or a click outside closes the question and nothing is done. A row no bot holds closes as before. The **Close** on the chart's position line asks the same.

A row goodcryptoX could not close says why; if goodcryptoX cannot take them at the moment (it is starting, stopping or locked), the list says so and nothing was done. The same is in the API (`POST /api/v1/positions/close-all`). goodcryptoX had no close-all; the place at the end of the filter row is new.

## Counts and freshness

The figures that move with the market refresh about every 5 seconds, not on every price: a bot's unrealized PnL and its APR (the runtime works them out at the latest price and sends only the bots whose figure changed), and a position's mark, PnL, margin and liquidation price (they arrive with the exchange's own updates, about every 5 seconds). Anything that changes what a row *is* (an order filled, a bot's step, a position's size) shows at once. A list sorted by PnL re-orders at those refreshes, at most once every 5 seconds, and the row you have open stays where it is on the screen.

The tab titles read **Positions · Orders · Bots**, with no number or total on hover. Orders remembers Open or History when you visit another list. The chart resize handle sits directly below the chart, with space before the tabs. The list keeps enough height for its tabs, toolbar, column headings and one full row. When it sits below the chart, the chart gives up height first so the row and its Cancel button stay reachable.

If your goodcryptoX runtime cannot read a venue account, the list never pretends you have nothing: it says **Unavailable** and the rows shown are the last ones known. It says so only once a reading has ended without an answer, never while the first reading is still under way. If the reading is only late, the list is greyed with "as of" and the time of the last good reading. If the screen loses its connection to your runtime, "Runtime not reachable" shows and every action is off until it is back.

## Details in place

Click a row to open its details; click it again to close them.

- **On a phone** the details open under the row, in the page. The back gesture closes them. *Maximise* gives them the whole list area.
- **On a desktop** the details take the place of the list inside its pane, with *Back to the list*, which returns you to the list where you left it.
- An order's details have **What happened?** in their header only when the order ended in an error or a refusal, and a bot's when it has an error or something needing repair (not a position's, and not the list rows): it shows the story of that order or bot, one line per step, and lets you send it to goodcryptoX if you want help. See [Cases](../reference/cases/README.md).

An order's fee shows in two rows: **Exchange fee**, what the exchange took, and **Platform fee**, goodcryptoX's part; together they are the order's whole fee. When the exchange does not name the platform's part, one **Fee** row shows the whole fee.

The details stay open while the list changes around them. An order that fills while you look at it stays on screen with "No longer matches the filter", and its buttons change to what still applies.

The chart draws the order or bot whose details are open. A row on another instrument never switches the screen by itself: use its **Chart** button to move the book, the trades and the chart to that instrument.

An order's exits appear as a tree directly under its head, before the fields: each take-profit, stop-loss and rest send has its own status and opens its details. Before the entry fills, provisional legs show their shares and Waiting. A child's page links back through the parent card above it. A position shows its working exits in the same tree; completed exits stay in History. A bot's orders and positions link to the bot by name. The chart follows the child and its parent, or the position and its working exits.

Every head reads the same way: the title across the top (an order's side word green for buy, red for sell), your note, the status, and the buttons in the row under them. Sections start collapsed; open the ones you need.

- **An order**: its type (point at the type for what it does), size and how much has filled, the prices that apply to its type (**Limit price** on a limit, stop limit or trailing limit order, **Price** on a market order, trigger with its initial value, trailing distance, activation price, average fill price), the market price when it was created and the price improvement you got, fees and the modifications. An order placed outside goodcryptoX says **Source: Imported from exchange**. Last comes the **Event log**, always open: one row per event, its date over its time, then what happened (created, triggered, each fill, fully filled, replaced, canceled with what had filled). An order placed outside goodcryptoX starts with when it was placed on the exchange and its exchange id, then what goodcryptoX saw of it.
- **Your note** on an order or a position: in the head of its details, edited in place with its pencil (**+ Add a note** when there is none), plain text up to 500 characters, kept by your goodcryptoX (not in its trading records); a position's note stays with the account and coin when the position closes and a new one opens. Empty it and save to remove it. An order keeps its note when you change it. goodcryptoX 2.0 had notes on nothing; a bot's note is its own (its name and note).
- **A bot**: goodcryptoX 2.0's details page, described on [the DCA bot's page](../bots/dca-running.md#the-bots-details): status, parameters (changeable from the next cycle), profitability with the realized PnL cycle by cycle, its position next to the exchange's, when to stop (changeable at once, and Move TP to break-even), progress, the orders list, notifications, name and note, and the orders of each cycle. A bot that needs repair offers the repair choices instead of its usual buttons.
- **A position**: at the top its name and, at the right, **PnL** (the unrealized PnL); under them its size, large, green for a long and red for a short, with its value in dollars beside it ("≈ 470 USD"); under that your note (the margin mode and leverage are the Leverage row's), then Close, Add and Chart. Then plain rows with no sections: **Entry price**; **Liquidation**, with the distance bar the Positions list draws under it; **Mark price** with its time; **Leverage**, the setting as it stands with a pencil that opens the order form's own control (Isolated or Cross, the multipliers, and the small arrow for any other multiplier up to the market's maximum) with ✓ and ×; **Margin**; **Unrealized PnL**; **Realized since flat** (what the account realized on the coin since the position last opened from nothing or changed side); **Funding since open** (the exchange's own figure, received positive, paid negative). Each money figure carries its currency. Spot has no funding, leverage or margin.

An edit you leave without saving (another list tab, another row or bot, another page, or closing the details) is dropped, with nothing sent; coming back shows the order, bot or position as it is. When you edit a bot setting, a live update never overwrites what you are typing; if the runtime's value changes meanwhile, the field says so. A parameter's *Save* changes the bot at once; *Save from the next cycle* (a setting the cycle under way depends on) leaves that cycle as it is; nothing changes until you save. If the runtime refuses the change, your edit stays in the field and the details say why, so you can correct it and save again.

## Actions

- A take-profit or stop-loss whose order has not filled yet is not a row: the order's row shows it as +TP or +SL, and the order's details list it. When the order fills, it becomes a row of its own. Cancelling the order cancels them with it, and its row in History keeps the +TP and +SL it had planned.

- **Cancel** asks first (you can tick *Do not ask again*). The row then says "Cancel requested" until the venue confirms; the order stays in the list until then and cannot be cancelled twice.
- An open order's row has **Cancel** and **Chart**; it changes in its details ([Change an order](../trading/order-types.md#change-an-order)). An ended order's row (History) has **Repeat**, **Reverse** and **Chart**. **Repeat** and **Reverse** fill the manual order form with the same order, or the opposite side, without leaving the screen ([Copy](../trading/order-types.md#copy)); Reverse wears goodcryptoX's own circular-arrows icon.
- **Stop** a bot always asks, every time, in goodcryptoX 2.0's words: the bot cancels all its open orders and exits its position if *Close pos. on stop* is on. **Close pos.**, **Fix failed** and **Move TP to break-even** ask first too.
- **Close position** closes the whole position at market. You do not choose a size: your runtime sizes the order from the position when it sends it. It asks first; you can tick *Do not ask again*.
- **Increase** fills the form to add to a position on the same side.
- **Retry** and **Cancel** on an order of yours in repair ("Unknown:" and why), **Send now** and **Cancel** on one goodcryptoX holds unsent ("Held:" and why): each asks first ([What the status words mean](../concepts/states.md)). goodcryptoX 2.0 had no such state; the buttons are new. Retry, Send now and a bot's **Break-even** ask every time: their question has no *Do not ask again*.

An order that belongs to a bot has no Cancel: its **Bot** badge opens the bot, whose own buttons apply. An order that is only waiting, still being sent, or whose answer goodcryptoX is still checking has nothing confirmed on the venue to act on; it opens its details only. Closing the details also clears the order or bot from the chart.

The row you pressed is the row acted on, even if the list moves under your finger before you let go. The same holds for the buttons in the details: if the order is modified while you press, the confirmation names the order you pressed, and nothing is sent for it once the modification has replaced it: the row and its details say "The order changed; check it and try again."

After updating from an older build, saved list scopes reset once to all accounts and pairs. Your sorts, other filters and remembered confirmations stay. A narrower scope chosen in this version remains saved for its list.

Row actions use grey icons in rounded-square buttons: X closes a position or cancels an order, and candles open the chart. Hover identifies the action; bot status remains coloured at the start of its row.

Long position and PnL values keep their full text: focus the value and use the arrow keys, or scroll it horizontally. In a compact desktop Bots pane, hover over the row or tab to an action to reveal its buttons. On a phone, swipe the row.

When you narrow a desktop Positions pane, its values and Close action stay aligned under the headers.

Chart stays available on an open row for the current symbol. While a row’s selected view is on the chart, its Chart button is pressed: green on a faint green wash, the same in the details and on the Dashboard; press it again or use the form to return to the form’s drawings. While its details are open, Chart in the details and Chart on its row are one switch: both light when its lines are on the chart, a second press takes them off, and the details stay open.

In Bots, the name sits over DCA. Progress shows averaging on the first line and the cycle on the second. Position shows the size in its side’s colour over the grey approximate USD value. New browsers start with Time, newest first; each browser remembers a sort you choose.

In a position's details, **Leverage** is a row like Margin: its word, the setting as it stands (**Isolated 5x**) and a pencil. The pencil opens the order form's own control in place (Isolated or Cross, the multipliers, the small arrow for any other); a pick only changes what you are about to send, ✓ sends it to the exchange in one request and × closes with nothing sent. The row shows a small ring until the exchange shows the new setting, and if the exchange refuses, the control stays open and says why. The leverage and the margin mode are the account's on that coin, shared with anything else on it, a bot included: a running bot's later orders go at the new leverage. **Margin** has a pencil on an isolated position: type the margin you want and ✓ sends the difference in one request, adding or taking away margin; the exchange decides how much can be taken away and refuses more, and the row then says why. A cross position's margin is the account's and has no pencil. While goodcryptoX's figures from the exchange are out of date the rows are greyed, the Leverage pencil goes too; an open margin field waits until the figures are current again. Position TP/SL is not available in this release.

Known display limit: **Partly liquidated** is kept only for liquidations seen during this runtime session. Restarting clears that live-position tag, even within the last 24 hours; recorded full liquidation rows remain.

History search waits briefly while you type; recent results arrive a page at a time. Narrow Trade labels and wrapped detail action rows keep space between them; a requested position close keeps its waiting ring in place of Size.

Bots names stay centred with their row when narrow lists fold the name and instrument together.


The Search field follows goodcryptoX's quieter magnifier and close spacing. A menu marks the chosen row with a check; its fill follows the mouse or keyboard focus. Coin logos also load for balance-only assets and wrapped spot names. A logo that cannot be read keeps its built-in icon or letter circle.

The Bots status dot sits in the space before Time, centred between the list edge and the date.

In a narrow desktop pane an order row keeps its two lines, the type and size × price; the row is as tall as in a wide one.

ROE is the exchange's own figure; where it gives none, it is unrealized PnL divided by the margin at entry (size × entry price ÷ leverage). The Margin column shows the margin in use now.

Parent orders show separate attached-exit tags: **+3 TP** and **+SL**, or one tag when only one kind is attached.
The **ext** tag marks an order placed outside goodcryptoX; an order goodcryptoX placed never carries it.
A child's details show its order or position above, in a lighter card.
