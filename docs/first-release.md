## Why we moved

goodcryptoX ran on our servers: the candles you saw came from our servers, your orders and bots lived there. That business model was not sustainable, and 2.0 was shut down. You asked us to bring it back, so we built goodcryptoX 3.0, a version you run yourself. It needs no servers of ours, so it is sustainable, and you do not depend on us: once installed, it works for you even if we disappeared tomorrow. We develop it AI-first.

## What it is

goodcryptoX 3.0 is goodcryptoX self-hosted: it runs on your machine, or on a server you rent, and it is yours to run. For now, in the beta, we start with Hyperliquid: manual trading and the DCA bot. More will follow.

What each version brings is in the release notes: Settings › General › Version history in the app, and [goodcryptoX's releases](https://github.com/GoodCryptoApp/gcx/releases).

This is goodcryptoX 3.0 beta: we fix what you find as it comes, and updates come often. Once installed, on your Mac or on a server, it keeps itself up to date: new versions install by themselves, nothing for you to do. Support is where it always was: [t.me/goodcrypto](https://t.me/goodcrypto).

## How it works

goodcryptoX trades only while it runs, so once an order or a bot is in, you want it running day and night. Where it runs is up to you, and you can change your mind at any time.

The easy way to start is your Mac: install it, connect an account, see it work. A desktop Mac that is rarely off, with a reboot now and then, can be goodcryptoX's home for good. A laptop that travels and sleeps is a place to start, not to stay: while the computer is off or asleep, goodcryptoX is off too, and your bots go blind. Orders resting on the exchange still fill. A short break is fine: when the computer is back, goodcryptoX reads every position, order and fill and catches up.

When you are ready, move it to a [NAS at home](install/nas.md) or a [rented server](install/server.md): one file, a few minutes, accounts and bots included ([Moving goodcryptoX](install/moving.md)). Or start there: any computer sets up a server or a NAS, Windows and Linux included, and so does your phone alone. Apps for Windows and Linux are coming.

Wherever it runs, you use it in a browser, and its notifications reach you in Telegram. On your Mac, click the icon. On a server, open the first link once, then my.goodcrypto.app ([more](install/server.md#sign-in)).

## Secure and free to run

Free to run: no subscription, no limits. The one charge is 0.03 % of each filled trade goodcryptoX places for you (on spot, of each sell), collected by Hyperliquid on top of its own fees ([Terms of service](legal/terms.md)).

Your keys stay on your machine. They can trade, never withdraw.
