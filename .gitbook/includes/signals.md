### Start on a signal

<details>
<summary>TA rating</summary>

The TA rating combines 26 indicators into one rating for each candle period.
Choose **Strong Buy**, **Buy**, **Sell** or **Strong Sell**. Buy includes Strong Buy; Sell includes Strong Sell.
Tick the periods you want, such as 5m and 15m. The whole selection must agree at the same time: every ticked period, not just any one.
DCA can enter while the candle is still forming; Grid waits for the candle close.

</details>

<details>
<summary>RSI</summary>

RSI measures the strength of recent rises against falls over 14 candles.
**Oversold** means RSI is at or below 30 at the candle close; **Overbought** means at or above 70.
Choose the state and tick the candle periods you want. Every ticked period must meet it.

</details>

<details>
<summary>MACD</summary>

MACD compares fast and slow moving averages to show momentum, with fixed settings of 12, 26 and 9.
**Bullish cross** means MACD crosses above its signal line; **Bearish cross** means it crosses below.
The cross must be on the latest closed candle of every period you tick.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Bollinger bands</summary>

Bollinger bands sit two standard deviations above and below the 20-candle average, showing how far price is from that average.
Choose **Price ≤ lower band** for a close at or below the lower band, or **Price ≥ upper band** for a close at or above the upper band.
Every candle period you tick must meet the chosen condition.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Price move</summary>

Choose **Fell** or **Rose**, a positive percentage and a whole duration in minutes, hours or days.
**Fell 10% in 7 days** means within a week or any shorter stretch: price is at least 10% below the highest price since the wait began, looking back at most seven days.
For example, a move from 100 to 112 and then 100.8 qualifies: price fell 10% from 112.
**Rose** measures a rise from the lowest price in the same window.
Earlier moves do not count. Each DCA cycle begins a new wait; choose the duration to match the move you want to trade.

</details>
